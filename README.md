# Hold Your Breath

Lets you steady your aim while ADS with a keybind, calming BHS's arm-injury sway on demand.

Instead of the constant shake from a badly wounded arm, hold the key and your aim eases down to fully steady. Let go and it eases back, with a brief "out of breath" spike before settling to normal.

## How to Use

**Hold (or press to toggle) the keybind, Space by default, while aiming down sights.**

Sway eases down to steady over the Hold-In Time. Release the key and sway ramps up to a brief exaggerated spike, then eases back down to normal.

## Features

* **Real Native Sway, Not a Stand-in:** Modulates GAMMA's own BHS arm-injury sway effector directly, using the same power calculation BHS itself uses, rather than faking a similar-looking effect.
* **Smooth Hold-In:** Steadying eases in gradually instead of snapping to zero.
* **Out of Breath Release:** Letting go ramps up to a brief, exaggerated sway spike before easing back down to normal, simulating the strain of holding your breath.
* **Hold or Toggle:** Choose whether the key must be held down, or pressed once to toggle the effect on and off.
* **No Custom Exe Required:** Works on a stock Anomaly-based exe — no engine patch needed.

## Toggles & Options

* **Enable Mod:** Master switch for the whole mod.
* **Activation Mode:** Hold: sway is reduced only while the key is held down. Toggle: press once to start, press again to stop.
* **Keybind:** Key used to hold breath. Only active while aiming; its normal action works as usual otherwise.
* **Hold-In Time:** How long sway takes to ease down to fully steady after pressing the key.
* **Release Ramp Time:** How long the "out of breath" spike takes to ramp up right after releasing the key.
* **Release Spike:** How much bigger than normal the spike gets. Set to 1 to disable it.
* **Release Time:** How long the spike takes to ease back down to normal sway.

## Installation

Install the mod through **Mod Organizer 2** as you would any other Anomaly mod. Enable the mod in your MO2 left pane, then launch Anomaly.

## Compatibility

Requires GAMMA's BHS (Boredom's Hardcore Sandbox) limb-injury system with its "New Limb Penalties" feature enabled. This mod only has a visible effect while BHS's own arm-injury sway is active — that is, while arm health is below BHS's configured threshold. At full arm health, or with New Limb Penalties disabled, there's nothing to steady.
